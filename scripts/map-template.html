Every incident reported to the CER, one dot each. Colour shows the most serious incident type; larger ringed dots were classed as Significant. Drag to pan, scroll or use the buttons to zoom, hover a dot for details.

Read with care

Federal pipelines only. The CER regulates interprovincial and international lines. Most of Alberta's provincially regulated pipelines report to the AER and are not here.

More pipe, more dots. Clusters follow where the network is densest (NOVA in Alberta, Westcoast and Trans Mountain in BC), not necessarily where risk is highest.

Approximate locations. Coordinates are as reported; treat them as nearby, not exact.
